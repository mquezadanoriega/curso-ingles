S2: Present Perfect I

Presente perfecto — Estructura, verbos regulares e irregulares

Estructura

Afirmativo: Subject + have/has + past participle

Usamos el Present Perfect para hablar de experiencias de vida, acciones pasadas con relevancia presente, y acciones que comenzaron en el pasado y continúan.

"I have played tennis for 2 years."
"She has gone to the supermarket."

Verbos Regulares

Para los verbos regulares, simplemente agregamos -ed al final.

"I have walked to school today."
"They have studied English for 3 years."

S3: Present Perfect II

Negativas, preguntas, palabras temporales y FOR vs SINCE

Afirmativo, Negativas y Preguntas

Afirmativo: S + have/has + past participle

Pregunta: Have/Has + S + [ever/never] + past participle?

Negativo: S + have/has + not + [ever/never] + past participle

"Have you ever been to London?" / "I have never eaten snails."

Palabras Clave Temporales

PalabraUsoEjemplo
JUSTAcción reciente"I have just arrived."
ALREADYAntes de lo esperado"She has already finished."
YETPendiente (neg/questions)"He hasn't arrived yet."
STILLContinúa hasta el presente"I still haven't eaten."
"The meeting has already started but Josephine still hasn't arrived. I have just sent her a message but she hasn't received it yet."

FOR vs SINCE

FOR = período de tiempo (for 2 years, for a long time)

SINCE = momento específico (since 2015, since last Monday)

"I have been vegan for a long time."
"I have been vegan since 2015."

For how long have you been vegan?

Since when have you been vegan?

S5: Present Perfect Continuous

Relación entre pasado y presente — Enfoque en duración y tiempo transcurrido

Present Perfect Continuous

Muestra la relación entre el pasado y el presente, con enfoque en la duración y el tiempo transcurrido.

Estructura: Subject + has/have been + verb+ing + complement

"This is the moment we have been waiting for."
"This is the moment we haven't been waiting for."
"What have we been waiting for?"

Estructura de Preguntas

[wh] + have/has + subj. + been + verb+ing

"How long have you been waiting?"
"What has she been doing?"
Tip: Se usa cuando la acción es continua y queremos enfatizar cuánto tiempo ha durado, especialmente con "for" y "since".

S6: Modal Verbs II

May, Might, Shall, Ought To — Posibilidad, sugerencia y consejo

Modales — Función General

Los modales modifican la intención del verbo: probabilidad, habilidad, permiso, obligación.

Regla: siempre seguidos por BARE INFINITIVE (sin "to").

MAY — Posibilidad / Permiso formal

MAY = posibilidad o permiso formal

"You may say I'm a dreamer, but I'm not the only one." — John Lennon
"It may rain tomorrow."

MIGHT — Posibilidad

MIGHT = posibilidad (menos probable que MAY)

"I might be wrong."
"She might come to the party."

SHALL — Sugerencia

SHALL = sugerencia (usado principalmente en inglés británico)

"Shall we dance?"
"Shall I open the window?"

OUGHT TO — Consejo / Recomendación

OUGHT TO = consejo/recomendación (más formal que "should")

"You ought to know better."
"We ought to respect the environment."

S11: Common Mistakes

Errores comunes — Diferencias entre palabras similares y falsos amigos

DO vs MAKE

MAKE = manual / creación (make breakfast)

DO = actividades no vinculadas a la creación (do homework, do sport)

"If I make breakfast, will you do the dishes?"

SAY vs TELL

SAY = se enfoca en lo que se dice (SAY something)

TELL = se enfoca en quién lo dice, necesita objeto indirecto (TELL me/you/her + something)

"He told me that you often say inappropriate things at the dinner table."

OTHER vs ANOTHER

ANOTHER = uno más

OTHER = algo diferente

"Are there any other cafes close by? I'd like to get another coffee."

AS vs LIKE

AS = en el rol de alguien

LIKE = comparando con alguien/algo

"As a friend, I feel I should tell you that you look exactly like your mother in that dress."

Double Negative (Doble Negación)

Dos negaciones se cancelan entre sí.

Doble negación (INCORRECTO)Correcto
"She hardly studies""She almost doesn't study."
"We have no siblings""We don't have any siblings."
"I saw nobody outside""I didn't see anybody outside."
